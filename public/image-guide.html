Image assignment guide

These are the 18 photos now live in src/assets/images/ and shown on the site. The filename decides where each photo appears (hero, product cards, facility sections, gallery). If a photo is in the wrong place, rename this file on disk and the site updates automatically, or tell me which number is wrong and I will fix it.

Placement rule of thumb — hero-* → homepage hero · product-* → product cards · factory-*/machinery-* → capabilities + facility gallery · about-* → about sections. Every photo also appears in the gallery.
